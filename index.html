A letter from my heart
My dearest Sikriti,

I’ve been thinking a lot about what happened, and I don’t want to hide behind excuses or try to make my mistake sound smaller than it was. I lied to you about my past, and I understand that this hurt you deeply.

You trusted me, and I should have respected that trust by being honest with you from the beginning. I can understand why you feel hurt, disappointed, and perhaps unsure about what to believe now. You had every right to expect honesty from me. I’m truly sorry that my actions made you question that.

I made a big mistake. I know saying “I’m sorry” cannot undo it, and I don’t expect a few words to repair the hurt I caused. I’m not asking you to forget what happened or to pretend that everything is okay. I want to take responsibility for it and show you, through my actions, that I can be more honest, open, and deserving of your trust.

I love you, Sikriti. Not just because I miss having you beside me, but because you matter to me as a person. I want to be with you, share the ordinary days and the difficult ones, and build something where you never have to doubt my honesty.

I want another chance—not because I think I’m entitled to one, but because I genuinely want to rectify what I’ve done. If you’re willing, I want to listen to everything you feel, answer your questions truthfully, and give you the time and space you need. I know rebuilding trust will take patience and consistent actions, not promises made in one letter.

I also understand that the decision is yours. I won’t pressure you or rush you. Whatever you need right now, I will respect it. I only hope that, someday, my actions can help you feel safe with me again.

I am sorry for hurting you. I love you, and I wish I had handled things differently. I want to make things right, one honest step at a time.

With sincere apology, Safalya

This isn’t a request for an immediate answer. It’s simply my way of taking responsibility and telling you how much you mean to me.
